# Ribbits — Cozy Frog Villages and Musical Bands in Swamps

Ribbits 9minecraft Mod populates swamp biomes with friendly frog villagers, cozy settlements, and musical bands. These small frog folks love building tiny towns and playing music together in the water. The mod turns quiet wetlands into lively, welcoming places filled with color and charm. You can find them tending crops, casting magic spells, or playing fun tunes on tiny instruments.

Frog Villagers: Cute Ribbit mobs spawn naturally in swamp biomes and form peaceful communities.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **HellionGames**, **Joo5h**, **YUNGNICKYOUNG**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
